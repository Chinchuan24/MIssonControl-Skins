# Cyberpunk Skins add-on

Unlocks **Cyberpunk Edgerunner**, **Lucy · Edgerunners**, **Arasaka** and **Edgerunners 2** (and two secrets), with the pixel-art cast, Street Cred, Braindance (BIOCHIP and Roman's Tape on Edgerunners 2), the boot line and the sound pack in `assets/`. `skins/` holds the four skins as JSON, to copy as a starting point for your own.

Install: put this `cyberpunk` folder at `addons/cyberpunk/` inside your Mission Control folder, then run `/skin reload` (or restart) and `/skins`.

> Edgerunners 2 is a fan homage to the second season of Cyberpunk: Edgerunners (CD PROJEKT RED / Studio Trigger / Netflix): original drawing and pixel art only, episode titles and short phrases, no logos. All names and characters belong to their owners.
